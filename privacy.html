Privacy Policy

YT Autopost is a private application used only by its owner to upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access: only the owner's own YouTube channel, and only to upload videos, set thumbnails and read basic channel information. We do not access the data of any other person.

Data we store: an authorization token, kept locally on the owner's computer. It is not shared with anyone and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Sharing: we do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

YouTube: the application uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
